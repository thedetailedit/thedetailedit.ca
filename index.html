Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions.

What do you need from me to get started?

A little information about your business, goals, and existing branding. Photos, videos, and brand assets are helpful too.

Can you work with my existing branding?

Absolutely! We'll use your existing colours, fonts, and style to create content that feels like your business.

How involved do I need to be?

As involved as you'd like! We can find a balance between your input and having us take care of the details.

Will I have input on the content?

Of course! You'll have the opportunity to review and approve content before it's published.

Do you guarantee specific results?

Marketing takes time and consistency. While results can't be guaranteed, the focus is on thoughtful content that supports your business goals.

Can I book a one-time project?

Absolutely! You don't need an ongoing package. Individual projects and one-time support are welcome.

Do you offer customized support?

Yes. Every business has different needs. We can discuss your priorities and put together an approach tailored to your business.
